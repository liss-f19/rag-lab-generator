Write markdown with:

- a level-1 heading repeating the lab title,
- `## What this lab teaches`: three to six bullets, each naming one mechanism and what the student
  must be able to do with it,
- `## APIs`: a table-free list, one line per function, `name` - what it does - the man page to read,

- `## Pitfalls`: three to five one-line warnings taken from the outline.

Use only what the outline contains: do not add functions, flags or facts that are not there. Keep it
under 400 words and write it in English.
